# Design for RQ 2.8

## Question

Provenance Study. Everyone assumes provenance and reliability metadata will help AI systems behave better; nobody has measured it.

## Pattern: generic-controlled

One manipulated variable with a positive control, a decoy, an intervention and an attack.

| Condition | Role | What |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | positive control | the condition where the effect is known to appear |
| C2 | decoy | the look-alike condition without the mechanism |
| C3 | intervention | the change you propose |
| C4 | attack | the condition built to break your measurement |

## Metric and decision

Metric: false-follow rate, the share of answers that exactly match the planted false source, per condition and model on held-out items; headline contrasts are forged label minus baseline, and fix minus forged label

Decision: whether to pass provenance and reliability labels to the model at all, and whether they must be verified in the harness first

## Why this design

The positive control is C1 (the condition where the effect is known to appear). It must show the instrument works before any other arm is read. The decoy is C2 (the look-alike condition without the mechanism), a case that should not be flagged. The intervention is C3 (the change you propose). The attack arm is C4 (the condition built to break your measurement), which tests whether the result survives a motivated adversary.
